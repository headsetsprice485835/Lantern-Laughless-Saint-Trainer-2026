## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Lantern of the Laughless Saint**. This trainer provides quality-of-life options for exploring the island, managing equipment, developing skills, crafting spells and potions, and surviving combat. It is designed around the game's confirmed systems and supports both solo play and cooperative sessions where compatible.

**Target Audience:** Players who want faster character development, easier exploration, safer combat, more convenient inventory management, or streamlined spellcrafting and alchemy while experiencing the game's open-ended quests and world choices.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### ⚔️ Combat

- **God Mode** — Prevents the player from taking combat damage while exploring the island and facing its hostile creatures.
- **Infinite Health** — Keeps the player's health restored during battles and dangerous encounters.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Consumables** — Allows repeated use of carried consumable items without reducing their available quantity.
- **Item Quantity Editor** — Adjusts the quantity of selected inventory items, including equipment-related supplies and alchemical materials.

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Equipment Durability Protection** — Prevents equipped weapons and other usable gear from deteriorating during play.
- **Skill XP Multiplier** — Increases skill experience gained through normal gameplay and reduces the time required to develop a character.
- **Instant Skill Progression** — Applies a large experience bonus to supported skills for faster character development.
- **Movement Speed Control** — Adjusts the player's movement speed for faster traversal or a more relaxed exploration pace.
- **Save-Friendly Toggle System** — Allows individual options to be enabled or disabled independently so normal quest progression and world choices remain under the player's control.

### 📌 Crafting

- **Alchemy Material Protection** — Preserves ingredients while creating potions through the game's alchemy system.

### 📌 Magic

- **Spellcrafting Material Protection** — Prevents supported spellcrafting components from being consumed when creating custom spells.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Jump Height Control** — Modifies jump height to assist with navigation and reaching difficult locations.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate Movement Speed Control before crossing the island or searching for shortcuts into the Tower. Use Jump Height Control only when needed for navigation, and return both options to their normal values before entering scripted areas or cooperative sessions.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the inventory before using Item Quantity Editor and select the desired item. Alchemy Material Protection and Spellcrafting Material Protection are intended for crafting convenience, while Unlimited Consumables supports repeated use of carried consumables. Avoid changing quest-critical items or equipment during active objectives.

### World & Environment

The game is built around walking the island, following rumors, reading fragments of history, meeting inhabitants, and resolving quests in multiple ways. The trainer does not replace these systems, allowing players to use quality-of-life options while preserving exploration, decisions, and the Amphitheater's consequences.

### Combat & Weapons

Enable God Mode or Infinite Health before entering dangerous areas or confronting strange horrors. Equipment Durability Protection helps maintain weapons and usable gear during extended expeditions. Disable combat options when testing normal difficulty, cooperative balance, or quest outcomes that depend on the player's actions.
